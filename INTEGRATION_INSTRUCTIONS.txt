https://github.com/swmezon/drone-gnc-6dof

Extract these folders/files into the ROOT of the existing repository.

It adds:
  src/attitude_controller.py
  src/closed_loop_simulation.py
  src/control_metrics.py
  src/attitude_visualization.py
  scripts/run_closed_loop_attitude.py
  tests/test_attitude_controller.py
  tests/test_closed_loop_attitude.py
  docs/CLOSED_LOOP_ATTITUDE_CONTROL.md
  README_CLOSED_LOOP_SECTION.md

It does not overwrite your existing plant-model source files.

  python -m scripts.run_closed_loop_attitude
  pytest -v

Then copy the contents of README_CLOSED_LOOP_SECTION.md into README.md.
